# 💰 Doc2HTML: Modern Monetization & Marketing Strategy (2025-2026)

## The Shift: From Flat Subscriptions to Hybrid Value

The SaaS landscape has evolved. Today's successful micro-SaaS apps don't just charge a flat monthly fee; they align their pricing with the **actual value** and **AI compute** consumed.

### 1. **Hybrid Pricing Model (Sub + Credits)** ⭐
- **Base Subscription**: Covers hosting, support, and basic usage.
- **Usage Credits**: Required for heavy lifting (AI processing, batch conversions).
- **Why?**: Protects your margins from high AI API costs while giving users flexibility.

### 2. **AI Add-ons: The Real Revenue Driver**
- **AI Document Summarization**: $0.10 per doc.
- **Semantic Tagging for RAG**: $0.20 per doc (Optimizing HTML for LLM ingestion).
- **Automated Content Cleaning**: $0.05 per doc.

## STRATEGIC ADD-ONS (The "Upsell")

### AI-Ready Output (The RAG Edge)
Modern developers don't just want HTML; they want **Semantic HTML** that an LLM can easily parse.
- **Feature**: Auto-inject `<article>`, `<section>`, and `<nav>` tags based on document structure.
- **Price**: Included in Pro or $0.50 per manual conversion.

### Zero-Retention Privacy
In the age of AI, data privacy is the #1 concern.
- **Feature**: Guarantee that files are processed in-memory and never hit disk storage.
- **Target**: Legal, Medical, and Financial sectors.
